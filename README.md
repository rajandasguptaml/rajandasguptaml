## About

I am a software engineer with a research interest in applied machine learning. I currently build user-facing systems at [Apex DMIT Ltd](https://www.apexdmit.com/) and pursue an M.Sc. in Computer Science at [Jahangirnagar University](https://juniv.edu/), following a B.Sc. in CSE from [American International University–Bangladesh](https://www.aiub.edu/). My current focus is data analytics and machine learning, with a growing emphasis on natural language processing for clinical and biomedical text.

- Engineering interfaces and data products at Apex DMIT Ltd
- Preparing for doctoral research in clinical / biomedical NLP
- Open to collaboration on [Machine Learning Practices](https://github.com/rajandasguptaml/Machine-Learning-Practice) and [Competitive Programming](https://github.com/rajandasguptaml/Competitive-Programming)
- Reach me on **Programming, Machine Learning, and applied AI** topics
